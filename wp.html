Advancing Biobehavioral Health Research

Investigating psychological adjustment and coping in chronic disease and cancer survivorship through innovative, interdisciplinary research at UC Irvine.

Cancer Survivorship

Improving quality of life and long-term outcomes for cancer survivors through evidence-based biobehavioral interventions and support strategies.

Chronic Disease Adaptation

Understanding how individuals adapt psychologically and behaviorally to chronic health conditions, with focus on resilience and coping mechanisms.

Biobehavioral Processes

Integrating psychological, behavioral, and biological perspectives to understand health-related adversity and develop targeted interventions.
